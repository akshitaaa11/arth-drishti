# Phase 5 models (Tier 1 sector, Tier 2 stock)

Generated by `make train` (`src/bsp/models/run_models.py`). Same walk-forward harness, hold-out lock, metrics and costs as `reports/baselines.md`; every number is out-of-sample. Nothing here is investment advice.

## Setup

- Test budgets: 12 full budgets, FY2012-13 to FY2023-24. A training row needs fiscal year < t, `label_end <= day 0` of t and a complete target. Optuna tuning and every inner validation fold use training rows only (inner expanding walk-forward). Locked hold-out FY2024-25, FY2025-26 and sealed live FY2026-27 were not loaded.
- **Tier 1, `lgbm_ranker`:** LightGBM LambdaRank (shallow trees, depth <= 3), relevance = quintile grade of the 12m excess return within each budget, Optuna TPE with 15 seeded trials per fold maximising the inner walk-forward IC (the untuned defaults are always trial 0). Missing values are left to LightGBM's native NaN handling; no row is dropped. Expected excess return comes from a companion shallow LightGBM regressor; `P(beat Nifty 500)` = Phi(expected / sigma), sigma and the 80% interval from inner out-of-fold residuals (a normal approximation, not a calibrated probability).
- **Tier 1, `bayes_hier`:** PyMC hierarchical regression, sector-specific partially pooled slopes on the budget surprise (`alloc_vs_prev_re_pct`), partially pooled intercepts, pooled slopes on 12m momentum and execution credibility. NUTS, 2 chains x 500 draws, seeded. Predictions are posterior predictive: mean, 80% credible interval, P(excess > 0). A missing predictor is the training median (0 after standardising).
- **Tier 1, `tier1_blend`:** equal-weight mean of the two models' within-budget rank percentiles (and of their forecasts). This pre-specified blend is the Tier-1 output that feeds the Tier-2 ranker.
- **Tier 1, `tier1_stack`:** the stacked ensemble. Same two models, but the weight on the LightGBM ranker (grid 0, .25, .5, .75, 1) is learned for each test budget from the earlier out-of-sample folds whose 12m label was already resolved on its day 0 (highest mean IC; 0.5 until three such folds exist). Weights per budget: `reports/models_stack_weights.csv`.
- **Tier 2, `tier2_ranker`:** one LightGBM LambdaRank model for all stocks with query groups budget x sector (so it learns to rank inside a sector), features = the 11 stock features + the sector's out-of-sample Tier-1 blend output (`t1_rank_score`, `t1_expected`, `t1_p_beat`). Stocks are the listing-date plus liquidity universe (average daily traded value >= Rs 5 cr). Tier-1 output exists only from the first test budget, so stock training starts there and the first stock test budget is FY2015-16. **Survivorship bias is not removed** (today's 116 symbols, no delisted names): stock results are biased upward and must be read with that in mind.
- Portfolios: top 3 sectors / top 15 stocks, equal weight, 0.2% round trip, same windows and benchmark charging as Phase 4. IT Services is the control sector: headline numbers use the 14 budget-sensitive sectors (and the stocks in them).

The pooled test treats the same stocks in different years as independent, so it can over-flag; any alarm is therefore investigated (README Phase 5 exit criterion):

- Check 1, dates: every stock feature row has `as_of_date` <= its budget date: **yes** (938 rows). The stock features are price-based only (momentum, volatility, liquidity, beta, size) and the Tier-1 inputs are out-of-sample, so the control sector's budget allocation never enters a stock model.
- `ridge` (pooled rho 0.296, p 0.010). Check 2, label scramble: earlier predictions unchanged when later labels are scrambled: **yes**. Independent per-year test: mean IC of the control stocks 0.125 over 9 years, one-sided t-test p = 0.234. Context: 14.3% of the 14 budget-sensitive sectors have a pooled rho at least as large (median 0.153). **Explained, not leakage evidence:** no label or date leak path exists, and the effect is not significant once each year counts once; the pooled alarm is most likely stocks that stayed winners or losers across years (a hypothesis, not tested here) on a survivorship-biased list.
- `sector_then_momentum` (pooled rho 0.251, p 0.025). Check 2, label scramble: earlier predictions unchanged when later labels are scrambled: **yes**. Independent per-year test: mean IC of the control stocks 0.006 over 9 years, one-sided t-test p = 0.482. Context: 21.4% of the 14 budget-sensitive sectors have a pooled rho at least as large (median 0.067). **Explained, not leakage evidence:** no label or date leak path exists, and the effect is not significant once each year counts once; the pooled alarm is most likely stocks that stayed winners or losers across years (a hypothesis, not tested here) on a survivorship-biased list.

## Not built, and why

- **Company fundamentals (D12) and order-win extractor (D13 / F8).** No point-in-time source is reachable: Yahoo's fundamentals are a current snapshot (using them for 2012-2023 budgets would be look-ahead), and filings/exchange announcement archives need manual download. Nothing was estimated or typed in. Tier 2 therefore uses price-based stock features plus Tier-1 output only; the README's fundamentals and `OrderInflow / MarketCap` features are open.
- **LSTM / temporal CNN embedding (Could).** Not built (no deep-learning library in the project, and the README keeps it only if the ablation shows a gain; that ablation is Phase 8).
- **Stack over Tier 2 and the LSTM.** The stack covers the two Tier-1 models only.

## Exit criteria

- Per-year walk-forward results for every model vs every baseline: this file and `reports/models_per_year.csv`, `reports/models_stock_per_year.csv`.
- IT Services (control): sector level shows no alarm. Stock level: every alarm was investigated and explained (see above).
