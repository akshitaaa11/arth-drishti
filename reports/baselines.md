# Phase 4 baselines

Generated by `make backtest` (`src/bsp/evaluation/run_baselines.py`). Every number is out-of-sample: expanding-window walk-forward, a budget is only predicted from rows whose 12m label was already known on its day 0. Nothing here is investment advice.

## Setup

- Test budgets: full budgets from FY2012-13 to FY2023-24; interim budgets have no sector features and are not predicted or used for training stocks either. Training grows each year (minimum 3 budgets).
- **Locked hold-out: FY2024-25, FY2025-26 (including their interim budgets); sealed live year: FY2026-27.** They are not loaded by the evaluation panels and loading them raises `HoldoutLockedError` until Phase 9.
- Target: 12m excess return over the Nifty 500 (day after the budget to the next budget's day 0).
- Portfolios: equal weight, top 3 sectors / top 15 stocks, bought at the close of the day after the budget and sold at the target window's end; 0.2% round-trip cost per window; cash earns 0% between windows; Sharpe uses risk-free = 0 over the whole span including cash days.
- The **Nifty 500 benchmark** is bought and sold over the same windows with the same costs.
- Seed 42; two runs give identical files.
- Limitations: stock results carry **survivorship bias** (the stock list is today's list, and a stock with no price on the sell date is dropped); the Nifty 500 point-in-time universe is not available yet (listing-date filter only); sector baskets are thin in early years (see `reports/sector_basket_report.csv`); with 12 test years every statistic is noisy. A null result is a valid result.
- **Every baseline beats the Nifty 500 by a wide margin, mostly for structural reasons**: the baselines hold equal-weight baskets of today's (survivor) stocks, which tilts to small and mid caps, while the Nifty 500 is cap-weighted. The fair bar for a model is therefore the **equal-weight** row, not the Nifty 500.
- The stock ridge's high IC should be read with the same survivorship caveat: it is trained and tested on today's survivors, so size and liquidity features can look more predictive than they would on the true point-in-time universe. Re-run after the Nifty 500 history is added.

## Bars every real model must clear

From README section 12 (all out-of-sample):

- Mean Spearman IC across test years above 0.10: baselines score last_years_winners 0.081, biggest_allocation_increase 0.011, ridge 0.046.
- Top-3 sector basket Sharpe above Nifty 500, equal weight and last year's winners: Nifty 500 0.57, equal weight 1.19, last year's winners 1.41.
- Stock Precision@5 above 0.55 (baselines in the stock table above).
- 80% interval coverage between 75% and 85% (ridge row above).
